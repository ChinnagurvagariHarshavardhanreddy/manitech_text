linux description

Linux is a powerful, open-source operating system (OS) based on Unix,
created by Linus Torvalds in 1991. Known for its versatility,
security, and stability, it powers everything from the world's 500 most powerful supercomputers to smartphones,
servers, and embedded
Due to its open-source nature, hundreds of variations—known as distributions or "distros" like Ubuntu, Fedora, and Mint—are available, offering flexible,
cost-effective, and secure computing
environments for developers and end-users alike.
